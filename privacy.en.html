Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Sift is an iOS app that saves the content you come across on Instagram, organizes it for you, and brings it back when it is useful. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the app, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

Summary

  • What you save lives on your device first.
  • AI analysis runs on your device by default; if a cloud model is needed instead, you are told during onboarding.
  • You can use the app without an account. Your data syncs across devices only once you sign in.
  • There is no advertising tracking, no third-party analytics, and no sharing of your data for advertising.
  • Saved content (titles, links, notes) is never written to logs or crash reports.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all of your data from inside the app.

Data we process

Content you save

When you save a post to Sift we process: the content's link, its caption text, the thumbnail address, the short summary, tags and category produced by the AI, the date you saved it, its status (new / viewed / tried / archived), and any personal note you add.

Account information

On first launch the app creates an anonymous session whose identifier is a random string that does not identify you. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, the name and email address that provider shares with us is associated with your account. Sift never sees or stores your password.

Instagram link (only if you use saving by DM)

If you choose to connect your Instagram account, we store the app-scoped user ID Instagram gives us (the Instagram-scoped ID), your username, and the date you connected. This mapping is what lets content you DM reach the right archive. The step is entirely optional and can be undone at any time.

Technical data

Our server-side records contain anonymous outcomes only (for example "an item was saved", "a link was established"). Message text, captions, links and raw user identifiers are never written to these records. We do not collect advertising identifiers and do not track you across apps or sites.

How we use the data

  • To show you what you saved and make it searchable.
  • To categorize and summarize content automatically.
  • To sync your data across your devices, if you have an account.
  • To show an end-of-day reminder, if you allowed notifications.
  • To keep the service secure (for example, rate-limiting abuse).

We do not sell your data, do not share it for advertising, and do not allow anyone but you to reach your archive.

AI processing

Summarizing and categorizing content runs on your device's own AI model first, in which case the content never leaves the device. If your device does not support that model, or the model is temporarily unavailable, the work is done by Google's cloud model (Gemini via Firebase AI Logic). You are told about this possibility when you first open the app. Only the caption text of the item in question is sent for cloud processing, and it is not used to train models.

Saving by Instagram DM

When you DM a post to Sift's Instagram account (@hellosiftapp), that message travels through Meta's systems to our server. Our server reads the content link and caption from the message, creates an entry in your archive, and sends you a short confirmation reply.

This is a deliberate exception to the app's on-device principle: when you message a business account, the content passes through Meta and our server by design. That is why we state it plainly on the connect screen. AI analysis still runs on your device — what passes through the server is transport, not analysis.

Messages you send through Instagram are also subject to Meta's own privacy policy.

Storage and sharing

Your data is stored on your device and — if you have signed in — in an area reserved for your account on Google Firebase (Firestore). Security rules ensure that only your account can read its own data.

Your data is processed via the following providers:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, storage, server functions and (when needed) cloud AI.
  • Meta — only if you use saving by Instagram DM, to deliver those messages.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and notifications.

We share your data with no one else. Where a legal obligation arises, we may disclose data to the extent applicable law requires.

Retention

Saved content is kept until you delete it or close your account. Content you delete in the app is archived first; deleting your account removes everything permanently. The Instagram link mapping is deleted when you unlink or delete your account.

Your rights and how to delete your data

You can access, correct and delete your data from within the app at any time. Under applicable law (GDPR / Turkey's KVKK) you also have the right to access your data, request its correction or erasure, and object to processing.

To delete all of your data:

  • In the app, go to Profile → Account & sync → Delete account. This permanently removes all of your data both on the device and on the server, and cannot be undone.
  • To remove only the Instagram link, send unlink as a DM to @hellosiftapp. Your archive stays; new posts you send are no longer saved.
  • If you no longer have access to the app, write to us at the address below to request deletion.

Children's privacy

Sift is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn that we have collected such data, we delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When a significant change happens we will say so in the app; the date at the top of this page always reflects the version in force.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, write to SIFT_CONTACT_EMAIL or reach us at @hellosiftapp. We respond to requests within 30 days at the latest.
