Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Sift is an iOS app that saves the content you come across on Instagram, organizes it for you, and brings it back when it is useful. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the app, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

Data controller: Yasin Çetin, Türkiye. Contact: hellosiftapp@gmail.com. "We" in this policy means that controller.

Summary

  • What you save lives on your device first.
  • AI analysis runs on your device if your device supports it, and in the cloud if it does not; you are told which during onboarding.
  • You can use the app without an account. Your data syncs across devices only once you sign in.
  • There is no advertising tracking, no third-party analytics, and no sharing of your data for advertising.
  • Saved content (titles, links, notes) is never written to logs or crash reports.
  • Notifications are scheduled on your device only; we run no notification server.
  • Saving by Instagram DM is an optional route and, by its nature, means messaging an Instagram account — details below.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all of your data from inside the app.

Data we process

Content you save

When you save a post to Sift we process: the content's link, its caption text, the thumbnail address, the short summary, tags and category produced by the AI, the date you saved it, its status (new / viewed / tried / archived), the reminder date if you set one for it, and any personal note you add.

To fetch an item's title, description and thumbnail, the app requests the link directly from your device; no server of ours sits in between.

If you change the category the AI picked, that correction (old category, new category, date) is recorded inside your own account. We keep it only to measure how accurate the classification is and to improve it for you over time.

Account information

On first launch the app creates an anonymous session whose identifier is a random string that does not identify you. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, the name and email address that provider shares with us is associated with your account. Sift never sees or stores your password.

Until you sign in, none of your content leaves your device: an anonymous session does not sync, and your archive stays local. Sync begins the moment you sign in with Apple or Google, and everything saved up to that point moves into your account.

Instagram link (only if you use saving by DM)

If you choose to connect your Instagram account, we store the app-scoped user ID Instagram gives us (the Instagram-scoped ID), your username, and the date you connected. This mapping is what lets content you DM reach the right archive. The step is entirely optional, can be undone at any time, and requires a permanent account (Sign in with Apple or Google) — the incoming content has to land in an account.

While connecting, the app generates a six-digit, single-use code; it expires after 15 minutes and is deleted the moment it is used. To limit abuse, we temporarily keep a count of failed code attempts per sender.

Technical data

Our server-side records contain anonymous outcomes only (for example "an item was saved", "a link was established"). Message text, captions, links and raw user identifiers are never written to these records. We do not collect advertising identifiers and do not track you across apps or sites.

To verify that requests reaching our server really come from the Sift app, we use Apple's App Attest (via Firebase App Check). This proves the device is running the app; it does not identify you.

How we use the data

  • To show you what you saved and make it searchable.
  • To categorize and summarize content automatically.
  • To sync your data across your devices, if you have an account.
  • To show the end-of-day summary and any reminders you set on individual items, if you allowed notifications.
  • To keep the service secure (for example, rate-limiting abuse).

We do not sell your data, do not share it for advertising, and do not allow anyone but you to reach your archive.

Legal basis

We process your personal data on the following legal bases (GDPR Art. 6 / Turkey's KVKK Art. 5):

  • Performance of a contract — storing what you save, organizing it, making it searchable, and syncing it if you have an account. Without this the app cannot do its job.
  • Consent — saving by Instagram DM (connecting your account) and notifications. Both are optional; you can withdraw consent at any time (disconnect, turn off notification permission), and withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Legitimate interests — protecting the service from abuse (rate-limiting failed codes, verifying that requests really come from the app) and measuring how accurate the classification is.
  • Legal obligation — where the law expressly requires it.

Where your data is processed

Our server functions run in Google Cloud's European region (europe-west1). Our providers, however — Google, Apple, and Meta if you use the Instagram feature — operate global infrastructure, so your data may also be processed in countries outside Türkiye and Europe.

Those transfers take place under the standard contractual clauses and data processing terms of the providers concerned. If you would rather your data did not travel abroad, you have two options: used without signing in, nothing leaves your device, and you never have to use the Instagram feature.

Security

Your data travels between your device and our servers over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS) and is stored on your device under iOS's own protections. On the server, security rules ensure that only your account can reach its own data, and no client can read the Instagram mapping records at all. Every request from Instagram is signature-verified, and access tokens are rotated regularly.

No system is entirely without risk. If a breach affects the security of your data, we will notify the competent authority within the period the law requires, and tell you as well where it affects you.

AI processing

Summarizing and categorizing content happens one of two ways, and your device decides which. If your device supports Apple's own AI model (iPhone 15 Pro and later), the work is done on the device and the content never leaves it. If your device does not support that model, or the model is temporarily unavailable, the work is done by Google's cloud model (Gemini via Firebase AI Logic) — this is the route on older devices. You are told about this when you first open the app. What is sent for cloud processing is limited to the item's caption text and the name of the account that posted it — not your personal note, your account details, or the rest of your archive — and it is not used to train models.

All the AI does is categorize and summarize content. No automated decision is made about you that produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you, and you can correct the category and tags it produces at any time.

Search

In-app search runs entirely on your device. A numerical search index is built from the text of what you save and stored on the device; your search queries are never sent to any server.

What you save is also added to your device's system index so it can be found in iOS Spotlight. That index stays on your device, and anything you archive or delete is removed from it too.

Notifications

Sift uses local notifications only: they are scheduled on your device and fire there. We have no notification server, send no remote notifications, and collect no device push token. If you never grant notification permission, the rest of the app works exactly the same.

Saving by Instagram DM

When you DM a post to Sift's Instagram account (@siftsave), that message travels through Meta's systems to our server. Our server reads the content link and caption from the message, creates an entry in your archive, and sends you a short confirmation reply.

This is a deliberate exception to the app's on-device principle: when you message a business account, the content passes through Meta and our server by design. That is why we state it plainly on the connect screen. AI analysis still runs on your device — what passes through the server is transport, not analysis.

Inbox visibility

This needs saying plainly: @siftsave is an Instagram account, and messages sent to it appear in that account's inbox — exactly as they would with any business account you write to. So the person operating the account can technically see what you send. Instagram's API does not change this; no app can hide an account's incoming messages from the account's own owner.

That is why we commit to the following: the account is operated only by the person who builds Sift and is protected with two-factor authentication; the inbox is opened only for support and troubleshooting; message content is never exported, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or profiling. We suggest not sending anything by DM you would not want seen — the in-app share extension (Sift from the share sheet) does the same job and touches no server.

The note you send by DM

A plain text message you send within 90 seconds of sharing an item becomes that item's note in your archive (either order counts, since Instagram's share sheet lets you type before or after picking the content). The window is single-use and never overwrites an existing note. It is deliberately short: we do not want ordinary conversation landing in your archive.

Messages outside that window are not stored. We reply to them — for example with instructions if you have not connected an account yet — but their content is written nowhere.

Messages you send through Instagram are also subject to Meta's own privacy policy.

Storage and sharing

Your data is stored on your device and — if you have signed in — in an area reserved for your account on Google Firebase (Firestore). Security rules ensure that only your account can read its own data.

Your data is processed via the following providers:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, storage, server functions and (when needed) cloud AI.
  • Meta — only if you use saving by Instagram DM, to deliver those messages.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and notifications.

We share your data with no one else. Where a legal obligation arises, we may disclose data to the extent applicable law requires.

Retention

Saved content is kept until you delete it or close your account. Content you delete in the app is archived first; deleting your account removes everything permanently. The Instagram link mapping is deleted immediately when you unlink; if you delete your account, a regular cleanup job removes it within 24 hours at the latest.

The short-lived records we keep on the server to operate the Instagram channel are these, and none of them carry message content: a pending link code (15 minutes), the note window record (90 seconds), a failed-code attempt counter, and the fact that a welcome message has already been sent.

Your rights and how to delete your data

You can access, correct and delete your data from within the app at any time. Under applicable law (GDPR / Turkey's KVKK Art. 11) you also have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and access it;
  • have it corrected if it is incomplete or inaccurate;
  • request its erasure or destruction;
  • request restriction of processing and object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a structured, commonly used format (data portability);
  • withdraw consent where processing relies on it;
  • ask that corrections or erasures be communicated to third parties the data was transferred to;
  • and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK), and in the European Economic Area the data protection authority of your country.

Send requests to the contact address above; we respond within 30 days at the latest. Where a request could expose someone else's data, we may need to verify your identity first.

To delete all of your data:

  • In the app, go to Profile → Account & sync → Delete account. This permanently removes all of your data both on the device and on the server, and cannot be undone.
  • To remove only the Instagram link, there are two ways: in the app, Profile → Connect Instagram → Disconnect, or send unlink as a DM to @siftsave. Either one deletes the mapping; your archive stays and new posts you send are no longer saved.
  • If you no longer have access to the app, write to us at the address below to request deletion.

Children's privacy

Sift is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. In some countries the age at which you can consent to digital services on your own is higher (up to 16); below that age you should use the app only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If we learn that we have collected such data, we delete it — and if you believe we are processing a child's data, please write to us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When a significant change happens we will say so in the app; the date at the top of this page always reflects the version in force. If we ever need to widen processing that relies on consent, we will ask for your consent separately.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, write to hellosiftapp@gmail.com or reach us at @siftsave. We respond to requests within 30 days at the latest.
